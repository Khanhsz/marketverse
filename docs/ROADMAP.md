# MarketVerse — 16-Sprint Development Roadmap

**Sprint size:** target 2–4 hours of agent work for small scopes, variable for complex systems. The historical fast-track goal is two sprints/day (8 days) but is not a guarantee; automatic advancement is by quality gate, not clock.

## Milestones
| Phase | Sprints | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| A · Foundation | 01–04 | map interaction + correct weekly decision state |
| B · Economic core | 05–08 | sell, produce, transport, finance and compete for 16 weeks |
| C · Business depth | 09–12 | dynamic product, marketing, HR, analytic and macro systems |
| D · Alpha candidate | 13–16 | 8 cities, 4 AI, 104-week campaign, validated & polished |

## Detailed briefs
- [Sprint 01 — Foundation & Data Schema](sprints/01.md): Ensure the already committed scaffold is genuinely playable.
- [Sprint 02 — Country Strategy Map](sprints/02.md): Replace static centered Pixi world with a camera-controlled large interactive map.
- [Sprint 03 — City, Company and Facility Interactions](sprints/03.md): Bring game entities to life on the map.
- [Sprint 04 — Weekly Orders and Project Queues](sprints/04.md): Turn calendar advancement into a real weekly planning controller.
- [Sprint 05 — Customer Segments & Market Demand](sprints/05.md): Create market potential and demand allocation for product categories.
- [Sprint 06 — Production, Inventory & Logistics](sprints/06.md): Constrain revenue by what companies can make and deliver.
- [Sprint 07 — Finance, Ledger & Cash Flow](sprints/07.md): Create financially coherent company operations.
- [Sprint 08 — Competitive AI & Playable Core](sprints/08.md): Make strategy meaningful against real rivals.
- [Sprint 09 — Product Design and R&D](sprints/09.md): Support product differentiation and lifecycle decisions.
- [Sprint 10 — Marketing, Brand and HR](sprints/10.md): Create capabilities with delayed strategic effects.
- [Sprint 11 — Weekly Reports and Market Analytics](sprints/11.md): Make outcomes explainable from the map.
- [Sprint 12 — Macroeconomy and Market Events](sprints/12.md): Implement dynamic but credible economic change.
- [Sprint 13 — National Campaign](sprints/13.md): Scale to playable Alpha country and progression.
- [Sprint 14 — Balancing & Automated Scenarios](sprints/14.md): Stress test strategy viability and game economy.
- [Sprint 15 — UX Polish & Tutorial](sprints/15.md): Make the game legible and efficient on laptops.
- [Sprint 16 — Alpha Integration & Release Candidate](sprints/16.md): Deliver a verifiable playable Alpha, not a mockup.

## Sprint transition policy
Read `docs/AUTOPILOT_PROTOCOL.md`. A sprint is accepted only after evidence and UI smoke (when needed), then state is updated. If Work/Codex is no longer running, a later authorized session resumes from state. No implicit launch of AI from a GitHub push.

## Priority order
Core weekly correctness -> economic causality -> AI competition -> map UX -> content scale -> polish. Favor small playable increments with green tests over speculative breadth.
